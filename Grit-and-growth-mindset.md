# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

Grit means working hard and staying focused on a goal, even when things are difficult. Success does not depend only on talent or IQ; it also depends on patience, effort, and never giving up. Grit is commonly described as perseverance and passion for long-term goals despite obstacles and setbacks.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

There are two types of mindsets:

- **Fixed Mindset:** Believing that our skills and abilities cannot change.
- **Growth Mindset:** Believing that we can improve through practice, effort, and learning from mistakes.

A growth mindset is the belief that abilities and intelligence can be developed through effort, practice, and resilience. [1]

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

Internal locus of control means believing that our actions and decisions can change our lives.

For example, if I fail to solve a coding problem, I should find my mistake and try again instead of blaming others or my situation.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

- Believe in yourself.
- Learn from your mistakes.
- Accept new challenges.
- Ask questions and clear your doubts.
- Make a learning plan.
- Never stop learning.

## 5. My Ideas to Build a Growth Mindset

As a future software developer, I will follow these habits:

- I will practice coding every day.
- I will learn from my mistakes.
- I will not give up when I face difficult problems.
- I will learn new technologies step by step.
- I will not compare myself with others.
- I will be patient and keep learning.

By following these habits, I can improve my skills, become more confident, and grow as a software developer.
